City after dark

Real 4K aerial footage from Pexels with a grade, a drone HUD and a title sequence on top.

Footage: Stunning Aerial Timelapse of City Lights at Night by Kelly on Pexels (Pexels License). Everything on top of it, the grade, the HUD and the titles, was written by the session; the footage is not redistributed here.

The prompt

Given word for word to a fresh Claude Code session in an empty project made by kairoframe init. The session wrote index.html; nothing was edited by hand.

Make a 12-second cinematic opening title over real footage. assets/city.mp4 is a 13.5-second 1080p aerial timelapse of a city at night (from Pexels, "Stunning Aerial Timelapse of City Lights at Night" by Kelly, Pexels License); use it as the full-frame background from 0 s, with a slow push-in (scale about 1.0 to 1.08 over the film). Give it a cinematic night grade with KairoFrame's colour grading (see `kairoframe docs`): deeper shadows, warm highlights, teal in the darks, and a vignette. At 0.4 s letterbox bars slide in to a 2.39:1 frame. At 1.5 s a thin drone-style HUD fades in: corner brackets, a small REC dot, a timecode that runs fast (it is a timelapse: from 23:40:00 to about 02:10:00 over the film), altitude "ALT 412 m" with a slowly changing value, and a compass strip at the top that drifts. At 3 s the title "AFTER DARK" appears in the centre: wide letter spacing that tightens, each letter resolving from a soft blur, with a light sweep crossing it at 5 s; a subtitle "A city that never switches off" fades in under it at 4 s. At 9 s the title and subtitle drift up and fade, and at 10 s a small credit fades in at the bottom: "Footage: Kelly on Pexels  ·  Rendered with KairoFrame". Use a refined condensed sans-serif from Google Fonts for the title and a monospace font for the HUD. The footage must stay the star: keep overlays thin and semi-transparent.

Work in this folder (a kairoframe project made by `kairoframe init`; read AGENTS.md). Check your work with `kairoframe check` and look at `kairoframe sheet`; fix what is wrong. Do not run `kairoframe render`: the film will be rendered separately.

Make it yourself

1. Install KairoFrame and Claude Code
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aisnote/kairoframe/main/install.sh | bash
2. Start an empty project and save the prompt above as prompt.txt in it
kairoframe init city-after-dark --duration 12
cd city-after-dark
2b. Download the footage from Pexels and put the same 13.5 s at 1080p in assets/
# download the 4K file from https://www.pexels.com/video/stunning-aerial-timelapse-of-city-lights-at-night-33637800/
ffmpeg -ss 1.0 -t 13.5 -i <downloaded 4K file>.mp4 -an -vf "scale=1920:1080:flags=lanczos,fps=24" -c:v libx264 -crf 17 -pix_fmt yuv420p assets/city.mp4
3. Let Claude write the film
claude -p "$(cat prompt.txt)" --model opus \
  --permission-mode acceptEdits \
  --allowedTools "Read,Write,Edit,Glob,Grep,Bash(kairoframe:*),Bash(ls:*)"
4. Render
kairoframe render -o city-after-dark.mp4 --crf 23

Or skip steps 2 and 3: download this sample's index.html into a folder (with the footage from step 2b in assets/) and run step 4 there. A new session writes a different film each time; the cost of a run lands in the same range.
