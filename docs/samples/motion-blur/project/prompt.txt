The right panel's cards carry data-kf-fx="motion-blur(samples: 16, shutter: 180)" and the left panel's do not; everything else is the same. Use a dark studio background with a subtle floor reflection. Add a small caption at the bottom: "data-kf-fx=\"motion-blur(samples: 16, shutter: 180)\"" in a monospace font.
