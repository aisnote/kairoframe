original.html in this folder is the complete source of a public CodePen pen (MIT licensed): "Magic text" by SarahC (https://codepen.io/SarahC/pen/xXjEMP). Port it to this kairoframe project as a 10-second film at 1920x1080 that looks as much like the original as possible. The original runs on its own clock (requestAnimationFrame, timers, Math.random); in the film every frame must depend only on the film time: drive it from the GSAP timeline or the seek hook described in `kairoframe docs`, and replace Math.random with a seeded generator. Keep the particle count, colours and motion of the original. Particle state must be computed from the time (for example by simulating from 0 up to the requested time with a fixed step), so that any frame can be drawn on its own. Keep the original's MIT license notice as a comment at the top of index.html ("Based on 'Magic text' by SarahC, https://codepen.io/SarahC/pen/xXjEMP, MIT License"), and put a small, unobtrusive credit in the bottom-right corner of the film: "Magic text — SarahC (CodePen, MIT)".
